Supply, demand and stocks

For traders, refiners and analysts: how production, exports and stocks have moved together.

Production, exports and stocks (million tonnes, monthly)
How to read this

When stocks (the dark line) climb, more oil is being made than shipped or used at home. When they fall, the country is drawing down its tanks. Production is seasonal — it usually peaks around September to October and dips in the first quarter.

Latest month’s balance derived
What does this mean?

Starting from last month’s stock, add what was produced and imported, subtract what is left — the rest was used: shipped abroad or consumed at home. Stock cover says how many months of exports the stock could fill.

Where palm oil is grown and made

Pick a measure to colour the map. For planters and agronomists: compare your state with the national figure.

Map
What am I looking at?

Production is crude palm oil made, oil extraction rate is how much oil the mills get from each tonne of fruit, FFB received is fruit delivered to mills, yield is fruit harvested per hectare on estates, and planted area is land under oil palm. Colours on rate maps show who is above (green) or below (orange) the national figure.

States Perlis, Melaka, Pulau Pinang, KL, Putrajaya and Labuan are reported together as “Other states” in most MPOB tables, so they are grey on maps that need them separately. Boundaries: geoBoundaries (ODbL), simplified.

What is El Niño and why does it matter?

El Niño years bring dry weather to South-East Asia. Palms react slowly — the drop in fruit usually shows up 9–12 months later. Biodiesel mandates (such as B50) use up palm oil that would otherwise be exported, so stocks shrink faster.

Sources and limits

MPOB / BEPI (production, stocks, exports, yield, area, prices), OpenDOSM (industrial production index, used as a cross-check). Boundaries from geoBoundaries. MPOB lists data purchase rates, so check with them before redistributing the data. Source code, tests and method notes are in the project repository.

Figures come from public MPOB and DOSM releases and may be revised. “Derived” means calculated here, not published. Not investment advice.
