# Social post images

Five 1080x1350 post images (saved at 2x, 2160x2700), each a live capture of one dashboard page with a headline
taken from the data. **Figures are as of the data to September 2026 and the price to 29 October 2026**; they go
stale, so regenerate rather than reuse them later.

| File | Dashboard page | Headline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-outlook-el-nino-lag.png` | Outlook | 9-12 months | The what-if simulator; marked "illustrative, not a forecast". Simulator figures are assumptions, not a forecast. |
| `02-overview-stocks.png` | Overview | +49% in a year | Stocks 3.51 million tonnes, exports -22%, production +15% (year on year). |
| `03-markets-stocks-chart.png` | Markets | 3.51 million tonnes | Highest stock level in our records, which start in 2021. |
| `04-regions-borneo.png` | Regions | 46% from Borneo | Sabah and Sarawak share of September 2026 crude palm oil production. |
| `05-data-issues.png` | Data & methods | 23 known data issues | 12 from the source, 6 ours, 5 both; all 13 datasets fresh at capture. |

Sources: MPOB / BEPI and DOSM. The images were composed from screenshots of the running dashboard; the generator
scripts are not part of the repository.
